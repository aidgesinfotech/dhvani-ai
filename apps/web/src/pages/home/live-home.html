Voice & Speech

01

Native-language speech collected from diverse speakers for speech recognition, conversational AI, and voice assistants.

Real-world example
Read prompts and spontaneous conversation in Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil and other languages, recorded in homes, streets and vehicles.
Collection method
Scripted and guided recording on contributors' phones; studio sessions where acoustic control matters.
AI use case
Speech recognition (ASR), text-to-speech voices, wake words and voice assistants.

Images

02

Photos of real objects, places and scenes, captured in varied lighting, angles and environments, then labelled.

Real-world example
Street scenes, retail shelves, household items and food photographed across different cities.
Collection method
Guided capture tasks with framing and quality checklists, followed by box, polygon or class labels.
AI use case
Object detection, visual search and product recognition.

Video

03

Short clips of everyday activities and interactions, recorded to a script and segmented by action.

Real-world example
First-person clips of cooking, shopping or commuting; gesture and hand-object interaction recordings.
Collection method
Phone or head-mounted camera recordings with timestamped action segments added in review.
AI use case
Action recognition, video understanding and embodied AI.

Human Preference

05

Side-by-side judgements of model outputs by trained reviewers, each with a written reason.

Real-world example
Choosing the better of two chatbot answers for accuracy, helpfulness and safety.
Collection method
Pairwise ranking and rubric scoring, with overlapping reviewers to measure agreement.
AI use case
RLHF reward models, model evaluation and red-teaming.

Geospatial Data

06

Location-tagged observations collected on the ground by contributors who are physically there.

Real-world example
Storefront and signage photos, points of interest and road conditions, each with GPS coordinates.
Collection method
Field tasks with GPS check-in, route walks and geo-tagged photo capture.
AI use case
Map and POI enrichment, local search, delivery and mobility models.

Phone capture at different angles and lighting, then box-level transcription and key-value tagging.
AI use case
OCR, document understanding and form or invoice extraction.

08

Paired data where speech, images, video and text describe the same moment and are aligned in time.

Quality Control

Quality Is Built Into Every Dataset

Quality isn't a final inspection. It is checked at seven points, from who we let in to what we ship, so large datasets stay consistent and reliable.

  1. 01

    Contributor Screening

    Language, device and skill tests before anyone joins a project.

    Screened
  2. 02

    Task Instructions

    Clear guidelines, examples and edge cases for every task type.

    Briefed
  3. 03

    Automated Checks

    Format, duration, noise, blur and duplicate checks on upload.

    Auto-pass
  4. 04

    Human Review

    Trained reviewers inspect each sample against the spec.

    Reviewed
  5. 05

    Multi-Level Validation

    Second-pass and expert validation on a sampled share of work.

    Validated
  6. 06

    Quality Scoring

    Per-sample and per-contributor scores drive rework and routing.

    Scored
  7. 07

    Final Dataset

    Only verified samples ship, with metadata and documentation.

    Shipped

Rework loop

A sample that fails any gate goes back to its contributor with specific feedback, and contributor scores decide who gets which tasks next.
